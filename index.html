Fifth year · site models

Your site, at any scale.

Put the square on your site anywhere in the old Sligo borough, choose a scale, and download the ground as a 3D print, a laser-cut contour kit or a contour plan. The buildings are shown here for context; the downloads carry only their footprints, numbered, with a table of heights.

Drag to turn, scroll to zoom. Click a building for its height.
1 · Your site

Drag the orange square onto your site, or double-click the map to jump it there.

2 · Make
3 · 3D print (terrain only)
Building footprints on the ground

Matte white PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 10–15% infill, on the Bambu P1S. Larger squares are split into equal tiles of 230 mm or less that share one level datum, so they sit flush side by side.

3 · Laser contour kit

Cut lines red, engraving blue, as SVG and DXF. The kit includes the contour plan with numbered buildings and the table of heights.

3 · Contour plan

PDF (plan, then the table of building heights), SVG, a DXF for CAD (contours and footprints at their real levels, ITM metres), the table as CSV, and a site pack for the Site Model Hub.
